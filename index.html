Our Mission

The St. Maurus Church Youth Ministry — Youth Serving Christ — exists to lead young people into a lifelong relationship with Jesus Christ and His Church. We strive to create a welcoming environment where youth can grow in faith, build authentic community, and discover their God-given purpose.

Through prayer, formation, service, and fellowship, we empower young disciples to live out their Catholic faith with joy and conviction in today's world.

Connected in Faith, Driven by Love, United in Christ.

Who We Are

We are the youth of St. Maurus Church, Mathare North Road, Nairobi. Our ministry brings together young people from across the parish to worship, serve and grow together — meeting every Sunday at St. Maurus Church, with our youth choir forming a central part of that gathering.

Our year is anchored by the Yearly Youth Retreat, along with team building activities such as our upcoming Nakuru Team Building Trip, outreach projects and fellowship events.

What We Believe

As a Catholic youth ministry, we are committed to the teachings of the Catholic Church. We believe in:

The Holy Trinity

One God in three divine Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

The Sacraments

Especially the Eucharist as the source and summit of our faith.

The Church

As the Body of Christ, entrusted with continuing His mission in the world.

Catholic Doctrine

As Catholic Christians, we hold to the teachings of the Catholic Church as expressed in the Catechism of the Catholic Church and guided by the Magisterium.

Core Beliefs

The Creed

We profess the Nicene Creed each Sunday, affirming our belief in the fundamental truths of Christianity.

Sacred Scripture and Tradition

We believe that God's revelation comes to us through both Sacred Scripture and Sacred Tradition, guided by the teaching authority of the Church.

The Sacraments

We celebrate seven sacraments as efficacious signs of grace, instituted by Christ and entrusted to the Church.

Moral Life

We strive to live according to the teachings of Christ, especially as expressed in the Beatitudes and the Ten Commandments.

Prayer

We believe in the importance of both personal and communal prayer as essential to our relationship with God.

Youth-Specific Approach

While holding firm to Catholic doctrine, we present these timeless truths in ways that are accessible and relevant to young people today. Our approach emphasises:

  • Authentic witness from youth leaders and peers
  • Practical application of faith to daily life
  • Open dialogue and questioning in a safe environment
  • Community support in living out one's faith

Common Questions

Who can participate in youth ministry?

Our youth ministry is open to all young people of the parish and beyond. You don't need to be a member of St. Maurus Church to participate — all are welcome!

When and where do you meet?

We meet every Sunday at St. Maurus Church, on Mathare North Road, Nairobi. Our youth choir also meets as part of that Sunday gathering. Exact times are shared in our WhatsApp group.

Is there a cost to participate?

Most of our regular programming is free. Some special events like the Yearly Youth Retreat or the Nakuru Team Building Trip may have a contribution, but we never want finances to be a barrier. Speak to our Welfare Secretary if you need support.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just bring yourself! If you have a Bible, you're welcome to bring it, but it's not required. Wear comfortable clothes as we often have active games.

How do I join the WhatsApp group?

Easy! Just tap the WhatsApp group link, or use the green WhatsApp button at the top of this page. You can also call 0112 818587 and we'll add you in.

What if I'm not Catholic?

You are absolutely welcome! Our youth ministry is open to everyone regardless of religious background. We'll share our Catholic faith with you, but there's no pressure at all. Come as you are!
